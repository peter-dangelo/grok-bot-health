Grok Bot Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Who uses this app

Grok Bot is a personal-use application operated by Peter D'Angelo. It is used only by its owner, for his own Google account. It is not offered to or used by anyone else.

What data is accessed

With the owner's authorization, Grok Bot uses the Google Health API with read-only scopes to access the owner's own sleep, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, and activity (step) data. The app does not write, modify, or delete any data in Google Health.

How data is used and stored

The data is used only to give the owner summaries and insights about his own health and fitness. It is stored only on the owner's private computer and is not published or made available to anyone else.

Sharing

Data obtained through Google APIs is never sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to build or train any generalized models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Grok Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke Grok Bot's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time.
