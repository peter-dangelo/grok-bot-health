Grok Bot

Grok Bot is a private, personal-use application operated by Peter D'Angelo. It connects to the Google Health API with read-only access so that its owner can review his own Fitbit sleep, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, and step data.

This app is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner, for his own account and his own data.
